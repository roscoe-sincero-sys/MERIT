# PR #239: Maryland review verification and implementation

The reviewer’s November 7, 2026 decisions were checked against the official sources below. The approval-only review requested no code changes. This update implements the owner’s policy and receipt-quality requests; it does not activate policy drafts or deploy to production.

## Verified decisions and corrections

| Review topic | Verification and implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Department notice | [Environment §6-304](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gen&section=6-304&enactments=false) ties the child notice to Department/local-department receipt and identifies parent/legal guardian and conditional residence-owner recipients. Child drafts use an explicit under-six range, separate from the application’s general child population. Historical child drafts retain the July–October 2022 and October 2022–December 2023 threshold periods. |
| Pregnancy and investigation scheduling | [COMAR 26.16.08.05](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/26.16.08.05) includes pregnancy, assessment-contact steps, telephone attempts, a first-class final letter and discretionary closure. Approved Maryland EBL eligibility creates a separate scheduling task, without inventing a numeric scheduling deadline. Unreachable-family closure requires documented contact/letter reasoning and leaves a conditional owner-closure-letter task. Its case access remains until that follow-up is completed or dismissed with evidence. |
| Definitions and specimen confirmation | [§6-801](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gen&section=6-801&enactments=false) and [COMAR definitions](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/26.16.08.03) support the venous/two-capillary distinction and the 84-day window. Maryland notice modes require explicit source review and conservative confirmation checks. Discordant/uncertain capillary evidence stays in review for venous/source clarification. A primary-contact flag alone cannot establish a legally responsible caregiver. |
| Property definition correction | The reviewer’s pre-1950 description is incomplete for current cases: §6-801(b) also covers applicable pre-1978 rentals from 2015, elections and exemptions. Ownership and affected-property eligibility therefore require reviewed source evidence, not a guess from a building-year flag. |
| Additional owner notices | [§6-846](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gen&section=6-846&enactments=false) includes affected-property residence/regular exposure and Department forms. It does not itself supply a ten-day interval. Affected-property drafts label that interval as an operational choice requiring approval. Owner completion requires named recipients, property/delivery details, the Department form/document reference and an explicit all-required-owners attestation. The activity date represents the last delivery. |
| Current reference value | [CDC’s current reference-value information](https://www.cdc.gov/lead-prevention/php/news-features/updates-blood-lead-reference-value.html) still states 3.5 µg/dL. The drafts use that value from 2024; statutory future CDC changes and their delayed applicability require a new approved effective-dated version. |
| Laboratory reporting | [COMAR 26.02.01.04](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/26.02.01.04) supplies a laboratory final-result clock, distinct from Department notification. Drafts separate next-business-day elevated reports and the two-week below-reference interval for Maryland residents 18 or younger. A completed late-report finding is dated when lateness was identified. Collection/analysis/message dates are not substituted for an unknown final-result date. Due-date receipt requires explicit verification of the approved close-of-business cutoff; no default cutoff hour is invented. MDE receipt alone does not prove a separate Baltimore City submission. |
| Missing information | [COMAR 26.02.01.03](https://regs.maryland.gov/us/md/exec/comar/26.02.01.03) assigns the specimen/request clocks to laboratories/drawing facilities, not MDE. Those clocks are not repurposed as MDE deadlines. Detection creates a completed finding and a separate pending **Missing Information Received** task; completion requires actual receipt date and source evidence. |

## Workflow and safety decisions

- Removed the withdrawn jurisdiction example from the active configuration guidance. Maryland templates are reviewable form defaults, not active seed data. Agency holiday dates and program approval remain explicit.
- Staff must verify age/pregnancy, relevant confirmation tests, legal recipient, ownership, rental eligibility/exemptions/elections and secondary-property exposure. The current data model cannot establish every legal fact automatically. Uncertain items remain visible for review rather than being presented as completed compliance.
- Owner notices are tracked as a reviewed recipient group. Staff list all required owners/addresses and each delivery’s evidence in the note or referenced documents, attest full coverage, and enter the last actual delivery date. This is not automated ownership discovery or individual landlord delivery verification.
- Department-approved notice form references are retained in the original activity and shown in history. Delivery sharing across rules requires compatible eligibility checklists and currently valid confirmation evidence. Unsuccessful attempts cannot complete notification.
- Manual entry can capture an earlier actual Department receipt time. CSV profiles may map an explicit `finalResultDate`; the worker normalizes it using the profile’s date formats while retaining the original value. HL7 uses an explicit final/corrected result-status date when available, never the message-date fallback.
- Staff timeline verification uses separate server-controlled columns and records the reviewer, time and immutable policy version. Imported JSON cannot claim staff verification, exclusions or cutoff decisions. Source-based exclusions and their original evidence remain distinguishable from on-time reporting.
- Missing mapped fields are not proof a laboratory omitted them from its original report. Required-field lists are configurable. Verify conditional pregnancy, Medicaid, differing draw-site details and additional Department requirements against the original source. After receiving information, apply the correction/release in Held records separately.
- Held/unmatched receipt findings survive later linkage to a patient without changing their detection/completion dates. Bulk task actions cannot overwrite receipt findings or mark information received without the evidence workflow.

## Validation

Regression scenarios cover the 84/85-day boundary, discordant capillary results, legal caregiver versus primary contact, owner scope/form evidence, final-result versus collection/analysis clocks, actual detection dates, pending/dated information receipt, unknown dates, imported verification spoofing, explicit close-of-business review, and post-closure owner-letter access retention/release. Existing patient activities, document uploads and their permission checks remain covered by the original suites.
